Archived applicants are applicants you’re not actively hiring right now. They aren’t deleted, and you can still see their info, messages, and history. You can also contact them or bring them back anytime.

 

Archiving keeps your hiring list clean and focused. Applicants usually get archived automatically if no one interacts with them for a while, or you can archive them manually.

 


 

Above is where you can find your archived applicants.

 

Learn how applicants are archived
Applicants are archived when their application becomes inactive. This can happen in three ways:

Automatically due to inactivity

 

If no one interacts with an applicant for a set number of days, Workstream automatically archives them.


By default, this is 30 days, but admins can change it (see Set up and manage inactivity auto-archiving below).

Automatically when a job is deleted

If a job posting is deleted, all applicants tied to that job are archived automatically.

Manually by a user

A hiring manager or HR user can manually archive an applicant by marking them as inactive.

 
 

Prevent applicants from being archived
Any interaction with an applicant keeps them active. Actions that reset inactivity include:

Reject the applicant

Mark the applicant as hired

Open the applicant’s profile

Send an email or SMS to the applicant

Unarchive the applicant

If none of these actions occur within the set number of days, the applicant is archived automatically.

Understand how match scores work
Each applicant has a match score shown as a percentage (e.g., 90%) in the ✨ Match % column on the Applicants page.

 

The score shows how closely their resume matches your job description. It's calculated based on:

Job requirements of the role

Applicant’s past roles and industries

Alignment between the applicant’s experience and the job

⚠️ If the resume is vague or missing details, the score might not reflect the applicant’s true potential. The model is reviewed regularly to reduce bias.


Read and interpret match scores
Go to the Applicants page.

Find the ✨ Match % column. You’ll see:

🟢 Green score – Indicates a strong match based on the information available in the resume.

⚪️ Gray score – Indicates a partial or lower match; some information aligns while other parts may be unclear or missing.

🚫 No score (faded or blank) – Insufficient data to generate a match score (e.g., incomplete resume or unclear job description).

Hover over the match score to view their match details.

### Manage your availability
Once scheduling stages are set up, interviewers must add their availability to the Workstream calendar.

Updated over 6 months ago
Managing your availability allows applicants to self-serve and select available interview slots at their convenience.

Adding availability 
In Team View, go to Scheduling.

Click and drag the time slots to select in blocks. The minimum block required to add is one.

Select the interviewer for these slots. If you’re assigning availability to someone else’s calendar, choose Someone else.

Note: You need the "View, create, and edit all HR calendars" permission to assign availability to another user. If you need access to this setting, reach out to your Super Admin.

Edit the time slot by selecting the time on the drop-down list. If you are assigning to Someone else, select the timezone by clicking the arrow down button.

Choose how often this slot repeats. Select Every week for a recurring slot.

Enter the maximum number of applicants allowed per slot.

Tip: To allow multiple applicants to attend interviews at the same time, increase this number. Only applicants from the same location and with the same interview duration can book the same slot.

Check the box if you want to make interviews exclusive to one location. If selected, only applicants from the same location will be able to book interviews at the same time.

Select specific locations or positions if you want to lock in the slots for specific locations or positions only. Otherwise, proceed to Step 10.

Select positions and stages to schedule per location.

Click OK and then Save.

Make changes to your availability 
In Team View › Scheduling, click on the existing schedule you wish to edit. The right panel should enable to you to edit your availability. 

Once done with your changes, click on Save.

Remove your availability
⚠️ Applicants cannot self-schedule their interviews until you add availability again.

Go to Scheduling › Manage availability. Click Delete availability.

Select the calendar you wish to delete by clicking the drop-down arrow. Then click the Yes, delete button.


### Set up your scheduling settings
Manage time zones, date ranges, notice periods, and calendar sync settings to control how applicants schedule interviews with you.

Updated over 6 months ago
In the team view, go to Scheduling.

In the upper-right corner of the calendar, select the gear ⚙️ icon to open the Scheduling Settings page.
​

Editing scheduling settings
 

In the Scheduling Settings page, you can manage when and how applicants can schedule time with you through Workstream:

 

Settings
Time Zone
What it does: Automatically detects and applies your local time zone for calendar events. Keep the toggle on to let Workstream detect your time zone automatically. 

To set this up manually:

Toggle off the Detect time zone automatically option.

Use the dropdown menu to select your preferred time zone.

Available Date Range
What it does: Sets how many days into the future applicants can see and schedule meetings.

Set this up: Enter the number of days. Applicants will only see open time slots within this range.

Minimum Scheduling Notice
What it does: Blocks applicants from booking meetings that start too soon.

Set this up: Enter how many hours of notice you need. For example, if you enter 4, applicants won't be able to book anything starting within the next 4 hours

Integration
Google Calendar
What it does: Connect your Google Calendar to automatically block off busy times and prevent double bookings in Workstream.

Set this up: Use this article to connect your Google Calendar and start syncing your availability.

Outlook Calendar
What it does: Connect your Outlook Calendar to automatically block off busy times and prevent double bookings in Workstream.

Set this up: Use this article to connect your Outlook Calendar and start syncing your availability.

After any updates are made in the settings, the page should automatically save the changes.

### Schedule interviews with applicants manually
Manually schedule interviews by choosing from availability, auto-sending links, or setting a specific day and time for applicants.

Updated over 6 months ago
In the team view, under the HIRING section, click on 'Applicants'.

Search for the applicant you would like to manually schedule an interview with.

Then select the 'Schedule Interview' button located at the right side of the row. This will open the 'Schedule interview' window.
​

Schedule interview window

Choose how you want to schedule:
​

Automatically

This option sends a message to the applicant with a link to schedule an interview based on your calendar availability.

Note: If you haven't set up your availability yet, this option will appear greyed out until availability has been set

Select from availability

Choose an available time slot from your calendar.

Note: If you haven't set up your availability yet, this option will appear greyed out until availability has been set

Set a specific day and time

Enter a day and time you've already agreed upon with the applicant. You can also select a different user's calendar to directly schedule the interview for that user.

Note: This option does not require the user to have availability, but this option cannot override an existing schedule
​

Select Preview notification to review what the applicant will see when you schedule the interview.

Select Schedule to confirm the interview.

### Reschedule or cancel the interview appointment
Learn how to reschedule or cancel interviews with applicants, view cancellation reasons, and manage next steps in the hiring process.

Updated over 3 months ago
Sometimes interviews need to be moved, canceled, or marked as complete. This article walks you through how to manage these changes in your Workstream account and where to view comments from applicants who cancel their interviews.
​

Manage interview reschedules or cancellations
Here’s how to manage interview updates in your account:

From Team View, go to Applicants.

Go to the Interviews tab. This is where your applicants with scheduled appointments will be listed.

Select the three-dot (•••) menu beside the applicant you want to manage.
​

Rescheduling or cancelling an interview with an applicant
  

Choose if you want to:

Request reschedule: Reschedule the interview with the applicant. Follow the steps in the Schedule interview with applicants manually section to complete the process

Cancel meeting: Cancel the interview. You can either reject the applicant or reschedule the meeting

Mark as complete:  Mark the applicant as "complete" for the scheduling stage. You’ll then have the option to either reject the applicant or move them to the next stage of the hiring process

Mark as no-show: Mark the applicant as a "no-show." You can then choose to reject them or reschedule the interview

 

See how applicants manage their interviews
Applicants manage interview times from the confirmation email or text they receive. As a manager, expect this behavior:

Select Reschedule: Applicant picks a new date, selects a time slot, and confirms. The new time replaces the previous appointment in the Interviews tab.

Select Cancel: Applicant confirms cancellation, adds a reason, and confirms Cancel Interview. The applicant returns to the previous stage they were in before the interview.

📌 Applicants don't need to log into Workstream to reschedule or cancel.


 

View applicant reschedule and cancellations
You can view when an applicant reschedules or cancels their interview, along with any comment they include.

 

View the reason for a canceled interview
Go to the Applicants page.

Find the applicant who canceled in the Applications or Interviews tab.

Hover over the text that says “[Applicant name] canceled their interview”.

The applicant’s reason (if provided) will appear in a tooltip.
​

You’ll also see a system message in the Messages tab showing the cancellation time and automated notifications sent.
 

View a rescheduled interview
Go to the Applicants page.

Find the applicant who rescheduled in the Interviews tab.

In the applicant's profile, look for the Interview card. It will show Rescheduled, along with the updated date and time.

The Status field will display as Confirmed if the new time has been accepted.

You can also see system updates and automated messages about the reschedule under the Messages tab.
